Secure and Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

Zhu Yufan

Computer Science PhD Student at the National University of Singapore

I develop secure systems for private large-model inference. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning systems and applied cryptography, with an emphasis on protecting both user data and model assets across untrusted infrastructure.

My current work spans homomorphic encryption, secure multiparty computation, LLM systems security, encrypted state-space models, and GPU acceleration.

Research Interests

Privacy-preserving machine learning · LLM systems security · Homomorphic encryption · Secure inference · Confidential computing
