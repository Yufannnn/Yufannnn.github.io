Zhu Yufan (朱宇凡)

Computer Science PhD Student at the National University of Singapore

I received my Bachelor of Computing in Computer Science (Highest Distinction), with a second major in Statistics, from the National University of Singapore (NUS) in 2025. My undergraduate studies were supported by the NUS Science and Technology Merit Scholarship.

I began my PhD in Computer Science at NUS in 2025 under the supervision of Professor Xiaokui Xiao, supported by the NUS Research Scholarship. My current research focuses on privacy and security in large language models, including detecting and sanitizing sensitive disclosures and developing efficient inference over encrypted data.

Research Interests
